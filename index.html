About Us

The media crew behind every campus story worth telling

MLN Alliance MediaCrew is the official production, design and marketing body of our college — built by students, for students who want to make things.

We exist to give students a real production environment: proper briefs, proper deadlines, and proper feedback. Every event, poster and campaign on campus passes through one of our three departments, which means every member works on live projects from day one instead of sitting through theory alone.

  • Real briefs from real college events, not simulated exercises
  • Mentorship from senior crew members across every department
  • A portfolio of published work by the time you graduate
